# Licensing and data policy

Licensing is evaluated per layer; permission in one layer does not grant permission in
another.

| Layer | Phase 2.5 position |
|---|---|
| Source code | No final licence has been selected or asserted. Owner decision required before public release. |
| Raw parliamentary source | Immutable and private locally. Formal source/licence review remains unresolved. Do not redistribute. |
| Processed corpus | Redistribution status is `pending_review`. Checksums and private analytical use do not imply publication rights. |
| Taxonomies/codebooks | Repository-authored Australian definitions are versioned. Future external codebooks require source, attribution, and licence review. |
| Human annotations | Not implemented. Future consent, ownership, privacy, access, and release policies are required. |
| LLM annotations | Not implemented. Future provider terms, input retention, output rights, disclosure, and review policies are required. |
| Synthetic demo data | May be published after review if genuinely synthetic and free of copied speech, secrets, and identifying local paths. |
| Screenshots/examples | Use fictional or clearly redistributable data; exclude unpublished labels, credentials, paths, and private metadata. |

Public metadata uses stable corpus UUIDs/slugs, never local filesystem paths. Credentials
stay in ignored environment files. Provenance URLs, attribution text, and licence notes
are descriptive records, not legal conclusions.

Unresolved questions are the source-code licence, upstream XML terms, processed-text
redistribution, quotation limits, attribution wording, human-annotation ownership, and
future model-provider terms. Obtain appropriate legal or institutional review before
publication.
